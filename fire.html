FAQs

Fire alarm questions

How often does my fire alarm system need inspection?

Most commercial systems require annual inspection and testing under local fire code, with some components (like sprinklers, where applicable) needing more frequent checks. We track your schedule and handle the paperwork.

Can you take over monitoring for a system someone else installed?

In most cases, yes. We'll evaluate your existing panel and devices and can typically transition monitoring without a full system replacement.

What happens when the alarm is triggered?

Our 24/7 UL-listed central station receives the signal immediately, verifies it, and dispatches local first responders while alerting you in real time.

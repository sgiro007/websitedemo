FAQs

CCTV questions

Can I view my cameras remotely?

Yes. Every install includes remote viewing set up on your phone or computer, so you can check in from anywhere.

What does "NDAA-compliant" mean, and why does it matter?

It means the camera hardware meets federal restrictions on certain foreign-manufactured surveillance equipment — required for government-adjacent contracts and increasingly preferred by commercial clients for security reasons.

How long is footage stored?

Storage length depends on your recording setup — we'll walk you through on-site versus cloud storage options and retention periods during your consultation.

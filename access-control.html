FAQs

Access control questions

Can employees use their phones instead of a badge?

Yes, mobile credentials are supported on most systems we install, alongside traditional cards and fobs.

Can I see who came in and when?

Yes. Access logs track entry by credential, so you have a record of who used which door and when.

Does this work with our existing alarm or CCTV system?

In most cases, yes — access control, burglar alarm, and CCTV can be integrated onto a single platform for unified property security.
